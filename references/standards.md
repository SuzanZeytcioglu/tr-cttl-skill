# Standards for Turkish technical text

This file names the references CTTL uses. Confirm each current record in the [TSE catalog](https://intweb.tse.org.tr/) before you cite a year or a TS number.

CTTL follows the public aims of these standards. It does not copy their text. It does not check an outline. It does not issue a conformity claim.

## Pick the reference from the text

| Text | Reference | Aim CTTL applies |
|---|---|---|
| Product instructions, labels, maintenance steps, warnings | IEC/IEEE 82079-1:2019, adopted in Europe as EN IEC/IEEE 82079-1:2020. TSE records a European adoption as a TS EN standard. | Information for use supports the safe and effective task. |
| Information for users of software | ISO/IEC/IEEE 26514:2022. It refers to IEC/IEEE 82079-1 for information for use. Confirm any TSE adoption. | The text gives the information the reader needs, in a form the reader can apply. |
| Safety content of a machinery handbook | ISO 20607:2019 and EN ISO 20607. Confirm TS EN ISO 20607. | The handbook states the hazards and the measures for safe use. |
| A term, a definition, or a glossary line | TS ISO 704, TS ISO 860, and TS ISO 1087. Entry layout for a standard follows ISO 10241-1. Confirm each current TSE edition. | One concept has one preferred designation. A definition states the superordinate concept and the delimiting characteristics. |
| A specification, a plan, or another life-cycle information item | ISO/IEC/IEEE 15289:2019. A later edition may replace it. Confirm the current edition and any TSE adoption. | The item states the current content that item type requires. |

A product manual that includes software uses 82079-1 for the product tasks and 26514 for the software information.

## Aims the rewriter applies

These aims are public. They are not quotations.

- One concept has one preferred designation in the text.
- Keep a designation that the applicable Turkish standard already fixes. Do not coin a second name.
- A definition is not circular. It names the broader concept and the traits that set this concept apart.
- Name the target group. A text for a general reader defines an expert term on first use.
- A step names the actor, the condition, the action, and the result the reader must know.
- A warning stands before the hazardous step. It names the hazard, the harm, and how to avoid the harm.
- State the current product or system. Do not explain the present by narrating a change.
- In prose, use SI units. In prose, the decimal mark is a comma. Leave a number inside code, an identifier, or a protocol field as written.

## Local checks

Sentence caps, the semicolon check, and the closed lists in `writing-rules.md` are CTTL checks. They support the aims above. They are not clauses of a TSE, ISO, IEC, or IEEE standard.
